# Changelog

## v1.0.0 · 29 Oct 2026

The first public release of the Pathfinder concept prototype.

- **12 screens:** Overview, Results & Benchmarks, Ask Pathfinder, Roadmap, Peer Practices, Initiatives, Countries, Outcomes, Board Report, Readiness, Advisor console and Product vision
- **Grounded AI analyst** with visible reasoning steps, cited survey items, inline tables and charts, and one-click actions (10 question types)
- **Live re-planning:** business-priority sliders re-rank the roadmap and the impact/effort matrix
- **Working interactions:** tick off milestones, create the acquisition-integration initiative, draft the CFO escalation, generate and export the board report, and map uploaded evidence to survey items
- **Two personas:** the client CPO view and the certifier advisor view
- **13-step guided demo** and deep links (`?tour=1`, `?ask=…`, `?report=1`)
- **Zero dependencies:** a custom SVG chart library, no build step, deployed on GitHub Pages
