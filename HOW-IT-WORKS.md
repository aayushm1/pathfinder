# How Pathfinder works (plain English)

## The idea in one line
Today, an annual HR assessment is like a **yearly medical check-up** for a company's HR: you get a report card, then you're on your own. Pathfinder is the **personal coach**. It keeps using those results all year to explain, plan, track, prove and prepare for next year.

## Who is in the story
- **Northstar Workplace Institute** is a (fictional) provider that assesses and benchmarks companies' people practices.
- **Helix Biosciences** is a (fictional) pharma company with 8,400 people in 12 countries.
- **Maria Lindqvist** is Helix's Chief People Officer.
- **James Okafor** is a Northstar advisor who supports Helix.

## What happens, step by step

**1. The results arrive (Overview, Results).**
Helix scores 76.6. That's above average, but below the best companies in four areas: Onboarding, Leadership, Wellbeing and Succession. Instead of a PDF, Maria gets a live dashboard comparing Helix with 46 similar pharma companies.

**2. She asks why (Ask Pathfinder).**
Maria types: *"Why did our Onboarding score drop?"* Pathfinder replies with three reasons: the standard added a new requirement, the buddy programme only exists in 2 countries, and an outdated policy document was rejected. Each reason links to the exact survey question it comes from, so Maria can trust and defend it.

**3. Pathfinder builds a plan (Roadmap).**
It proposes 6 initiatives, ordered by impact and effort. If Maria cares more about cutting costs, she moves a slider and the order changes. It also shows the expected score if the plan is delivered (about 80).

**4. The team executes (Initiatives, Countries).**
Each initiative has an owner, milestones and a country-by-country rollout. Pathfinder raises an alert when something slips (Wellbeing budgets are stuck in 3 countries) and drafts the email to the CFO. It also notices from HR data that 700 people from a recent acquisition aren't on any programme, and suggests a fix based on what another company did.

**5. It proves the impact (Outcomes, Board Report).**
In countries with the new onboarding, new hires leaving within 90 days dropped by 46%. Pathfinder turns that into money (about €2.3M a year) and builds a 5-slide board presentation in one click.

**6. Next year is easier (Readiness).**
Everything the team did during the year counts as evidence. 64% of next year's survey is already filled in, which saves about 6 weeks of work.

**7. The provider benefits too (Advisor view).**
James sees which clients are thriving, which are at risk (one client's usage dropped after their CPO left), and where to offer more help. Usage data becomes retention and growth for Northstar.

## Why it matters
- **For companies:** clear priorities, peer examples, less admin, and proof of impact for leadership.
- **For the provider:** clients use the product every week, so they renew, buy advisory and expand their scope. Over time the provider also learns *which actions actually work*, which no competitor knows.

## What's real and what's simulated
Everything you can click works, but the data is fictional and the AI answers are scripted for the demo. A real version would use an LLM that only answers from the client's own data and the anonymised benchmark, with the same source citations.
